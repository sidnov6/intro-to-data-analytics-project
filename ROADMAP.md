## Phase gates

### 1. Idea selection

Do not choose an idea only because it sounds interesting. Confirm that it has a defined outcome, usable data, a realistic unit of analysis, an evaluation plan, and a manageable story.

### 2. Data feasibility

Download a small real sample. Check fields, dates, missingness, class balance, join keys, access limits, licences, and whether the target is genuinely available. Record problems before building models.

### 3. Analysis design

Write the primary question, baseline, metric, split/evaluation method, and planned comparisons before inspecting final results. This reduces accidental result-chasing.

### 4. Results and communication

Separate what the evidence shows from what it merely suggests. Include sample sizes, uncertainty where appropriate, and limitations close to the claims they qualify.
