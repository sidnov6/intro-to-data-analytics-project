# Use-Case Selection Scorecard

Score only after reading the full proposal and checking at least a small sample of real data. Use **1 (poor), 3 (acceptable), or 5 (excellent)**; intermediate scores are allowed. Add evidence, not just numbers.

| Criterion | Weight | What a high score means |
|---|---:|---|
| Research-question clarity | 15% | One focused, answerable question with a defined unit and outcome |
| Data feasibility | 20% | Required data are accessible, lawful, sufficiently large, and joinable |
| Evaluation strength | 15% | Fair baseline, appropriate metric, and credible validation design |
| Course fit | 15% | Demonstrates an end-to-end analytics workflow at suitable depth |
| Deadline feasibility | 15% | A useful minimum version can be completed with time for writing and rehearsal |
| Explainability and team fit | 10% | Every teammate can understand and defend the approach |
| Relevance and originality | 10% | Clear value and a distinct angle without unsupported novelty claims |

Weighted total formula:

`clarity×0.15 + data×0.20 + evaluation×0.15 + course×0.15 + deadline×0.15 + team×0.10 + relevance×0.10`

The highest number is not an automatic decision. A proposal with an unresolved access, ethics, licence, or deadline risk cannot be selected until that risk is addressed.
