# Reports and Final Assets

This folder will contain the micropublication and the final figures/tables used to support it.

Each headline claim should link back to a reproducible result. The final document should state:

- the question and why it matters;
- the data, period, unit of analysis, and sample size;
- the method and baseline;
- the main result in plain language;
- uncertainty and robustness where appropriate;
- limitations and the exact scope of the conclusion; and
- data, code, and AI-assistance acknowledgements.

Do not manually change a model-generated number in the report. Correct the source analysis and regenerate the table or figure.
