## How to use these references

For each candidate idea, ask:

- Is the research question as clear as the examples?
- Can we obtain and explain the data?
- Is the analysis proportionate to the course and deadline?
- Can the whole team defend the decisions in Q&A?
- What will make our work distinct rather than a copy?

Record any future source in the selected use case first. After topic selection, move approved academic, data, and technical references into a project-specific source register without deleting the original proposal history.
