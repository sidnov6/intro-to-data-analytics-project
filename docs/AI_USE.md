## Rules

- Never upload confidential, personal, restricted, or licensed data to an AI tool without approval and a valid basis.
- Verify factual claims and source links against primary material.
- Review code, rerun it, and understand it before merging.
- Record substantial assistance with research design, code, analysis, interpretation, or writing.
- Follow the instructor's current AI policy; Canvas is authoritative.
